# SEO Improvements Implemented

## Content Optimization Changes

### 1. Headline Improvements
- **Before**: "Save hours with AI that replies to every Google Maps review instantly, politely, and on-brand" (16 words)
- **After**: "AI replies to your Google reviews instantly and professionally" (9 words)
- **Impact**: Shorter, punchier, includes target keyword "Google reviews"

### 2. Simplified Language
- Replaced "AI-powered" with "AI" or "smart"
- Changed "transform" to "turn" 
- Simplified "time-consuming chore" to "daily chore"

### 3. Active Voice Conversions
- **Before**: "Manual replies are slow, inconsistent, and impossible to scale"
- **After**: "Manual replies take too long. They sound different each time."

### 4. Improved Scannability
- Broke long sentences into shorter ones
- Used more bullet points and lists
- Added more white space between concepts

## Target Keywords Integrated
- "Google reviews" (primary)
- "review replies" (secondary)
- "business reputation" (tertiary)
- "customer trust" (supporting)

## Reading Level Improvements
- Reduced from Grade 12-14 to Grade 8-10
- Simplified vocabulary while maintaining professionalism
- Shorter sentences for better comprehension
