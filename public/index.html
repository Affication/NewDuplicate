Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about AI review management and local SEO.

How does AI review reply automation work?

SatisPro connects securely to your Google Business Profile via OAuth. When a review arrives, AI drafts a personalized, on-brand reply that can auto-post or wait for your approval.

Will AI responses sound robotic?

No. SatisPro learns your voice, services, and preferences so every response feels human and contextual.

How does SatisPro improve my local SEO ranking?

By optimizing your GBP content, encouraging more reviews, and keeping NAP data consistent, SatisPro increases your visibility in map-pack and "near me" searches.

Is my Google Business Profile data secure?

Yes. We use Google OAuth, never store your password, and let you revoke access any time.

Can I customize the AI responses?

Absolutely. Set brand voice, key phrases, and response rules—or edit any reply before it posts.
